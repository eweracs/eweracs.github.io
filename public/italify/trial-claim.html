Fetching your trial key…

This is your personal Italify licence key. It runs for from the moment you enter it in Glyphs.

Activate Italify

  1. Install Italify: Open Window → Plugin Manager in Glyphs, search for “Italify”, click Install, relaunch Glyphs.
  2. Copy the key above and open Glyph → Italify → Settings and Licences… in Glyphs.
  3. Paste the key into the licence field and click Add.

Questions? The handbook is a good place to start.
