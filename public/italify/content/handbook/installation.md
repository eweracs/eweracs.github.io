# Installation {#installation}

@lede Two clicks and a relaunch – then all of Italify is in place.

1. Unzip `Italify.zip` and double-click the bundle for your Glyphs version – `ItalifyG3.glyphsPlugin` for Glyphs 3, `ItalifyG4.glyphsPlugin` for Glyphs 4. Glyphs installs it and asks to restart.
2. After relaunching you have all of Italify: the *Italify* entry in the *Filter* menu, the *Italify Tagger* in the Edit View toolbar (shortcut [[C]]), and the *Italify Groups* palette in the Window sidebar.

Italify requires **Glyphs 3.2 or later** (use the G4 bundle in Glyphs 4).
