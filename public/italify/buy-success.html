While you wait

  1. Install Italify from the Plugin Manager in Glyphs.
  2. Open the email titled “Your Italify licence key” and copy the key. No email after a few minutes? Check your spam folder first, then just reply to your order confirmation for assistance.
  3. Enter the key in Italify’s settings in Glyphs: Glyph → Italify → Settings and Licences…, or the Licence… button in the filter dialogue. You enter it once on each Mac – renewals and anything you add to the licence later reach Italify by themselves.
  4. Added to an existing licence? Then there is nothing to enter: Italify picks it up within a day, or at once with Refresh in the same settings.
  5. Bought a time pass? It waits until you start it – Italify asks when it arrives, and Start Pass… in the same settings starts it whenever you are ready.
