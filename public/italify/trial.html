How it works

  1. Get your link. You receive a link by email, valid for one hour. Follow it to get your 48-hour trial key.
  2. Copy the key. The link opens a page with your personal licence key.
  3. Enter it in Glyphs. Open Glyph → Italify → Settings and Licences… and enter the key in the licence field. Full access to all features, and your trial starts then.

Don’t have the plugin yet? Italify is available directly from the Plugin Manager in Glyphs – open Window → Plugin Manager, search for “Italify”, click Install; your trial key unlocks it.

One trial per email address. When it ends, nothing breaks – your files stay untouched; the filter simply pauses until you license Italify.
